# Demo fixture license

The files under `demo/scenarios` are synthetic fixtures generated entirely by this repository's `scripts/generate_demo_assets.py`.

To the extent possible under law, the author dedicates these generated demo fixtures to the public domain under CC0 1.0. They contain no extracted company assets, third-party artwork, production geometry, or proprietary textures.

The FBX/USD files are lightweight structural placeholders for audit demonstrations. The PNG files are deterministic procedural color, normal-style, and roughness-style patterns; they are not production textures.
